What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device that help us deliver and improve our services. They perform functions such as maintaining sessions, storing preferences, and measuring usage analytics.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for secure login, account management, and navigation.
  • Performance Cookies: Collect anonymous data about how visitors use the Service to improve content and user experience.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences, such as language and dark mode choices.
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense to deliver relevant ads and to measure ad effectiveness.

Third-Party Cookies

Our partners, including Google, may set cookies to deliver personalized advertisements and track performance. For more information, review Google's Advertising Policies.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings by disabling non-essential cookies or clearing stored data. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect Service functionality. To opt out of personalized Google ads, visit Google Ads Settings.

Consent

By using the Service, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy. Where required by law, we display a cookie banner to obtain explicit consent for advertising cookies.
